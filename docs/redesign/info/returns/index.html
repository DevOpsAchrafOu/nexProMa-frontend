Returns and exchanges

You have 7 days after receiving your order to change your mind.

Changed your mind

  • Within 7 days after delivery (right of withdrawal, Moroccan law 31-08).
  • The product must be unused, in its original packaging, with all its accessories.
  • The return is at your expense.
  • Once we have checked the product, we exchange it or refund you.

Defective or wrong product

  • We exchange it or refund you, and the return is at our expense.
  • Tell us within 7 days, with a photo or a video of the problem.

Products that cannot be returned once opened

For hygiene reasons, the anti-snoring nose clip, the electric toothbrush and the ear cleaner cannot be returned once the packaging is opened, unless they are defective.

How to return a product

  • Send us a WhatsApp message with your name, the product and a photo.
  • We tell you how to send or hand over the product, and organise the exchange or refund with you.
